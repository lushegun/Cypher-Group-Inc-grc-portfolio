# Free GRC templates

Blank, reusable versions of the documents in this portfolio. Each one keeps the structure and the drafting rules of the finished example, with every organisation-specific detail replaced by a `[BRACKETED]` field.

|---|---|---|---|

## How to download

- **One file:** click its name, then the **Download raw file** button (↓) at the top right of the file view.
- **Everything:** go to the repository's **[Releases](../../../releases/latest)** page and download the templates bundle.

## How to use a template

1. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` field — or delete the clause if it does not apply.
2. Use only numbers your organisation can actually meet next month.
3. Have legal counsel review monitoring and privacy clauses for your jurisdiction.
4. Work through the **fill-in checklist** at the end of each template before issuing it.
