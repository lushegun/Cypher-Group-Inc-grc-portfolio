## Scoring method

- **Requirement score:** 0 = absent · 1 = partly in place or designed but not evidenced · 2 = met, with evidence.
- **Clause score** = sum of scores ÷ (2 × number of requirements).
- **Annex A score** = (Implemented × 1 + Partial × 0.5) ÷ applicable controls (from the SoA).
- **Weighted readiness** = 0.6 × clause score + 0.4 × Annex A score.
- **Gate override:** if any gate requirement (4.3, 6.1.2, 6.1.3, 9.2.1, 9.2.2, 9.3.1, 9.3.2, 9.3.3) scores below 2, the Stage 1 decision is **NO-GO** whatever the percentage.
- **Bands:** ≥ 85% with no gate failure = Ready for Stage 1 · 60–84% = Ready within 90 days · < 60% = Not ready.

## Fill-in checklist

- [ ] All 28 requirements are scored, and every score of 2 cites evidence an auditor could sample.
- [ ] No gate requirement is scored 2 on the strength of a draft or an unapproved document.
- [ ] Internal audit (9.2) and management review (9.3) are scored on what has actually happened, not on what is planned.
- [ ] Every gap has an owner (job title), an action ID and a due date that matches the certification roadmap.
- [ ] The Annex A score uses the counts from the current, approved SoA.
- [ ] The Stage 1 decision applies the gate override before the percentage.
