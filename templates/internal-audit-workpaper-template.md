## Rules to set before fieldwork

**The dating rule — decide it before you grade anything:**

| Situation | Grading |
|---|---|
| A management-system clause requirement is unmet | **Nonconformity now.** Clauses are not dated by a plan |
| A control's planned implementation date has **passed** and it is not implemented | **Nonconformity** |
| A control's planned date has **not** passed | **Opportunity for improvement**, stating the date it becomes a nonconformity |
| The failure also breaches a present-tense legal or contractual obligation | **Nonconformity now**, whatever the plan says |

**Sampling:**

- Build the population from a named source and **test it for completeness before selecting**. A population you cannot
  evidence makes the control untestable — that is a finding in itself, not a pass.
- Populations below about 25: test in full. Larger populations: state the size and the basis.
- Random selections use a **documented seed** so a reviewer can reproduce the sample.
- **Population zero: draw no sample, reach no operating conclusion.** Record "not performed — population zero" and test design alone.

**Ratings:** *Major* = a requirement absent or broken down, or significant doubt that the system achieves its results ·
*Minor* = an isolated lapse that does not undermine the requirement · *OFI* = improvement advice, not a nonconformity.

## Fill-in checklist

- [ ] Objective, criteria, population, sample, test steps, evidence, two results and a conclusion — every field completed.
- [ ] Design and operating effectiveness are tested and recorded **separately**.
- [ ] Every population states its source and how completeness was checked.
- [ ] Every random sample states its seed.
- [ ] Every finding ID raised here appears in the findings register with an owner, a response and a due date.
- [ ] No conclusion claims evidence the organisation's own records say does not exist.
- [ ] Any independence limitation is declared on the face of the audit, not buried.
