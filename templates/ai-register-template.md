## How to find the systems (do this before filling a single row)

| # | Source | What it finds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Known architecture and procurement records | The AI you already know about |
| 2 | **Twelve months of card and expense transactions** | AI tools bought on personal cards: the usual shadow AI |
| 3 | **Identity provider: OAuth grants and connected apps** | Tools holding a token against company data, paid or free |
| 4 | **Every supplier's answer to "does your service process our data with AI, including features we have not enabled?"** | AI switched on inside software you already own |
| 5 | One short conversation per business owner | Why each tool is used, and for what |

Source 1 finds what you knew. **Sources 2 to 4 find what you did not.** A register built only from source 1 is not complete.

## Rules that keep the register honest

- **The tier belongs to the system; every duty belongs to a role.** A transparency duty that falls on the supplier is not yours. Write it in "Duties that sit with the provider".
- **Tier follows the use, not the technology.** The same writing tool is minimal for emails and high-risk for ranking job applicants.
- **A settings toggle is not a training prohibition.** Only a contract clause or signed written confirmation counts as evidence.
- **Record banned uses per tool.** Onboarding checks tools; this column is what stops an approved tool drifting into a banned or high-risk use.
- **Scenario or planned systems are labelled as such**, and kept out of scores until they are real.
