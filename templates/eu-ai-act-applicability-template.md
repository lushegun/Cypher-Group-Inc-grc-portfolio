## Traps

- **Each Art. 50 duty names a role.** A transparency duty on the supplier is not a duty on you.
- **A deployer can become a provider** by using a general tool for a high-risk purpose (Art. 25(1)(c)). Record intended and banned uses.
- **"No high-risk system" is only as good as your inventory.** Ask every supplier whether AI features process your data.
- **Delayed high-risk dates do not delay Art. 4, Art. 5 or Art. 50.**
- **The AI Act is not the only law.** Record GDPR duties (Arts 13-14 notice, 28 contract, 35 DPIA, 44-46 transfers) alongside.
