> **How to use this template.** Blank reusable template. Prepared by Olugboye Shola. One assessment per AI system, before first use,
> on any change of purpose or supplier terms, and every 12 months. It covers ISO/IEC 42001 clause 6.1.4 and A.5, and
> screens for a GDPR Article 35 DPIA in the same pass, so you do one exercise instead of two.

## The difference in one line

**A DPIA asks what happens to the personal data. An AI impact assessment asks what happens to the people who receive the output**, even when no personal data is involved.
